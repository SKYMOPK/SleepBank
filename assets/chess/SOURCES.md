# Chess appearance assets

- `preview.jpg` is generated by `node scripts/build-chess-assets.mjs` from
  `split-chess-scene.js`. It shows only the board and pieces; no ledger UI is
  baked into the thumbnail.
- The diffuse wood material reuses the existing CC0 Poly Haven wood image
  documented in `../homecoming/SOURCES.md`. A separate textured copy is not
  stored here.
- Chess scene geometry, lighting and motion are authored in
  `split-chess-scene.js`. Three.js is bundled locally under `assets/vendor/`
  with its MIT license.
